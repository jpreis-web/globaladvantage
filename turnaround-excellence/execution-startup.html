Control the field. Protect the objectives.

Execution transforms years of decisions into a compressed sequence of high-consequence activities.

EXECUTION / RESTART / CLOSEOUT
GLOBAL ADVANTAGE PARTNERSHIP

From shutdown to successful restart

Execution excellence depends on disciplined interfaces between operations, maintenance, contractors and project teams.

01

Safe shutdown & handover

Isolation, decontamination, system boundaries and readiness of workfronts.

02

Daily execution control

Short-interval schedules, constraint removal, progress integrity and issue escalation.

03

Quality & integrity

Inspection, test plans, quality records, punch control and mechanical completion.

04

Interface management

Coordination across operations, maintenance, contractors and CAPEX projects.

05

Recommissioning & startup

System restoration, safety-critical checks, operating readiness and stable return to service.

06

Closeout & improvement

Evidence-led reviews, performance analysis and lessons incorporated into future cycles.
