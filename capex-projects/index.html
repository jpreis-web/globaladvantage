CAPEX & PROJECT EXCELLENCE

Structure first. Delivery follows.

Industrial capital projects succeed or fail partly because of decisions made long before execution: who owns the decision, whether targets are realistic, how the delivery model is structured and whether readiness is proven at each gate.

GAP focuses on the governance, project organization, assurance and performance systems required for better investment delivery.

EARLY WARNING / INDEPENDENT OVERSIGHT

No surprises should be the ambition.

Strong governance is not achieved by receiving more reports. It requires management information that can be trusted, tested and acted upon while there is still time.

Independent assurance can challenge schedule realism, cost exposure, progress measurement, readiness, risk controls and emerging bottlenecks.

GLOBAL ADVANTAGE PARTNERSHIP

Questions worth asking

Practical answers to common decisions and concerns.

Do you focus on EPC delivery or project governance?

The emphasis is on investment strategy, delivery models, governance, stage gates, organizational effectiveness and independent assurance, with interfaces to execution and operations.

Are smaller MOC projects included?

Yes. Smaller industrial changes require a proportionate process with defined responsibilities, risk reviews and closeout controls, especially when they form a large portfolio.

What is the role of independent assurance?

An independent perspective tests the accuracy of reported progress and the credibility of cost, schedule, quality and readiness forecasts. Its value is early visibility rather than after-the-fact blame.
