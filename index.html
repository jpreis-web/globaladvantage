THE FOUNDATION OF TURNAROUND EXCELLENCE

Risk first.
At every decision.

A turnaround is an exercise into risk management.

The Risk-First Approach to Turnaround Management governs decisions about timing, scope, preparation, contracting, execution, startup and closeout. It is how anticipation becomes a management discipline and how difficult choices become more defensible.

Three golden rules of turnaround management. One discipline: identify tomorrow’s problems while there is still time to do something about them.
